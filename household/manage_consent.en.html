Consent this app asks for

Depending on your region and platform, the app may ask for:

  • Consent to personalized advertising (Google User Messaging Platform, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland)
  • Permission to use identifiers for tracking (App Tracking Transparency, on iOS)

Analytics is aggregate and tied to a random app-instance identifier; the account and cloud sync only come into play if you sign in yourself.

How to change your choice

  • In the app, open Settings › Privacy & legal › Manage consent to reopen the advertising consent form (shown where required).
  • On iOS, open the system Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking to change the tracking permission.
  • On Android, you can reset your advertising ID in the system Settings › Privacy › Ads.

If you decline

The app keeps working normally. You will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized, meaning they are based on general context rather than your activity.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Before ads load, a Google-certified consent message lists the advertising partners and purposes. Personalized ads are only used with your consent. You can view or withdraw consent through the Manage consent option described above.
