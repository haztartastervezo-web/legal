Manage consent and system permissions

Meal Planner / Heti Menü · Last updated: September 19, 2026

Google UMP – Android and iOS

The app follows the same core approach on Android and iOS: at each launch it refreshes consent information through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and shows Google's privacy form where required for the user's region and applicable purposes. Google Mobile Ads is enabled for ad requests only when UMP reports that ads can be requested.

Changing advertising choices

When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen Google's privacy form from the app's Settings / Privacy & legal area using “Manage consent”.

iOS App Tracking Transparency

ATT is an iOS system permission and is separate from Google UMP consent. You can later change the ATT permission in iOS privacy settings. If you deny ATT, IDFA is not sent in Google Mobile Ads ad requests; the app may still display ads, subject to the UMP state.

Other system permissions

Camera/photo access and notification permission are separate operating-system permissions, not advertising consent. They can be changed in system settings.

If you do not grant advertising consent

The core meal-planning features remain usable. The type or availability of ads depends on your region, UMP choices, iOS ATT state and Google's current advertising rules. We do not claim that every refusal automatically results in the same “non-personalised ads” mode.
