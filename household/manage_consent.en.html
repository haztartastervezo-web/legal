Google UMP — Android and iOS

At each app launch, the app requests an updated consent state from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where required, Google's privacy form is shown. Advertising requests are made only when UMP reports that ads can be requested.

Advertising and analytics consent

Google UMP can communicate applicable consent choices to Google Consent Mode, including ad storage, ad personalization, ad user data and analytics storage, when Consent Mode is enabled and configured for this app in Google's console. These choices affect how Google services may process data.

Changing advertising choices

When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen Google's privacy form from the app's Settings / Privacy & legal area using “Manage consent”.

iOS App Tracking Transparency

ATT is an iOS system permission separate from UMP. You can later change it in iOS privacy settings. If tracking is denied, IDFA is unavailable for ad requests.

Other permissions

Camera/photo and notification permissions are separate operating-system permissions and can be changed in system settings.

If you do not grant advertising consent

Core meal-planning features remain usable. The type or availability of ads depends on your region, UMP choices, iOS ATT state and Google's current advertising rules.
