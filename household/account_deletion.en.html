Account & data deletion

Meal Planner / Heti Menü · Last updated: September 24, 2026

Delete from within the app

If you are signed in, open Settings → User account → Delete account. After confirmation and, where required, recent re-authentication, the Firebase Authentication account is permanently deleted. If you use Sign in with Apple, the deletion flow also requests revocation of the Apple authorization token.

Server-side deletion of account-linked data

Firebase account deletion triggers the server-side account-data deletion process configured for the app. It recursively removes data stored under users/{uid}, including cooking-history subcollections, and is configured to discover and remove records whose designated userId field matches the deleted Firebase UID, such as account-linked nutrition correction reports.

This server-side deletion is asynchronous, so associated Firestore records may remain for a short technical period after the Authentication account itself has disappeared.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the app, email haztartastervezo@gmail.com with the subject “Meal Planner – account deletion request”. We will request only the minimum information needed to verify the account.

What is deleted?

  • the Firebase Authentication account and authentication record;
  • account-linked Firestore data configured for server-side UID deletion, including users/{uid} and matching designated UID fields;
  • other personal data directly associated with the account and controlled by us, unless retention is required by law.

What is not automatically deleted with the sign-in account?

  • Local app data: stored on your device; remove it in the app, through system app-data controls, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Google Drive appDataFolder backup: stored in your Google account; manage it through your Google account/Drive app-access controls.
  • Pseudonymous records not linked to the Firebase UID: for example raw poll votes or language-preference records identified only by an app-generated device UUID. These records are subject to their own retention/TTL period.
  • Aggregated statistics: anonymous aggregate counts may be retained where they no longer contain an identifier that relates them to a user or device.

Timing and retention

In-app deletion initiates Firebase Authentication deletion immediately after successful re-authentication. Server-side Firestore deletion and TTL deletion are asynchronous. External requests are handled without undue delay and within applicable legal time limits.
