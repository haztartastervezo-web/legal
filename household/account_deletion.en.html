Account & data deletion

Meal Planner / Heti Menü · Last updated: September 19, 2026

Delete from within the app

If you are signed in, open Settings → User account → Delete account. After confirmation and, where required by the provider, recent re-authentication, the Firebase Authentication account is permanently deleted. For Sign in with Apple, the app also initiates revocation of the Apple authentication authorization.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the app, email haztartastervezo@gmail.com with the subject “Meal Planner – account deletion request”. We will request only the minimum information needed to verify that the account belongs to you.

What is deleted?

  • the Firebase Authentication app account and its authentication record;
  • personal data directly associated with that account and controlled by us, if any, unless retention is required by law.

What is not automatically deleted with the sign-in account?

  • Local app data: this is stored on your device and can be removed in the app or by deleting the app's local data.
  • Google Drive appDataFolder backup: this is stored in your own Google account rather than storage controlled by us. It can be managed through your Google account's Drive/app-access settings.
  • Feedback: feedback records do not contain a Firebase UID or email address and therefore are not technically linked to the sign-in account. Every feedback record has a 90-day expiry followed by automatic TTL deletion. If you request earlier removal of a specific message and provide enough detail for us to identify it, we will make reasonable efforts to remove it.

Timing and retention

In-app deletion initiates account deletion immediately after successful authentication. External email requests are handled without undue delay and within applicable GDPR time limits. Data that must legally be retained would be kept only for the required period; the app currently does not process account-linked payment or billing data for which we specify a separate retention period.
