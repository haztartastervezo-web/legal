Privacy Policy

Meal Planner / Heti Menü (com.momreset.mealplanner) · Last updated: September 4, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Meal Planner (Heti Menü) app (package name com.momreset.mealplanner) (“the app”, “we”) handles information when you use it. The app is made by an independent developer based in the European Union. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Data controller

The data controller and provider of the app is Gábor Enikő, 1123 Budapest, Nagyenyed utca 5., Hungary.

Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device (in a local database and settings) and is not sent to us:

  • Recipes, weekly menu, shopping list, pantry (stock) items, food diary entries
  • Profile picture and food photos that you add
  • App settings (language, unit system, notifications, theme)
  • Your advertising consent choices

Account and sign-in (optional)

The core features work without an account. If you want cloud backup and sync, you can sign in with a Google or Apple account through Firebase Authentication. In that case we process:

  • The identifier, email address and display name provided by the sign-in provider
  • The content you sync (recipes, weekly menu, shopping list, food diary, settings), stored in Google Cloud Firestore so it can be restored on another device
  • When you send feedback, a question or a poll vote: your message plus device model, operating system version, platform, app version, language and a timestamp

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you start it, the app can back up your data to your own Google Drive account and restore it from there. The backup stays in your Drive storage; we cannot access it. This requires limited Google Drive access.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — showing ads; processing of device and advertising identifiers
  • Google Firebase Authentication — sign-in
  • Google Cloud Firestore — cloud storage and sync
  • Google Firebase Analytics — aggregate usage statistics
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications
  • Google Firebase Remote Config — remote feature configuration
  • Google / Apple Sign-In — authentication
  • Google Drive API — user-initiated backup

Advertising and tracking

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland a Google-certified consent message (Google User Messaging Platform, UMP) is shown before ads load, listing the advertising partners and purposes, where you can choose personalized or non-personalized ads.

On iOS, the system App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the app may use identifiers for cross-app tracking. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. See the Manage Consent page for details.

Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate usage (for example which screens are opened) so we can improve the app. This data is tied to a randomly generated app-instance identifier, not to your identity.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device (for example about the daily menu). We may also send push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging, for which the provider processes a device token. You can turn notifications off at any time in system settings.

Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for personalized advertising and, on iOS, for the use of identifiers for tracking; you can withdraw it at any time
  • Performance of a contract / service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to run the app and, when you use them, optional account, cloud sync, backup and feedback handling
  • Our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — non-personalized advertising that funds the app, security and stability, and aggregate usage analysis; you may object to this processing

Data security and retention

Local data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Cloud data is kept for as long as your account exists or until you ask us to delete it. Feedback messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your request. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data, including:

  • Access, correction and deletion
  • Data portability, and objection to or restriction of processing
  • Withdrawing consent at any time
  • Lodging a complaint with your data protection authority

In Hungary, the competent supervisory authority is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, naih.hu); you may also contact the authority in your own country of residence.

You can delete your account and cloud data directly in the app (using “Delete account” in the account settings), or contact us at the address below. California residents: we do not sell or “share” personal information for money or for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the ad choices described above.

International transfers

Google and Apple may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.
