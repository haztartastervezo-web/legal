Advertising, analytics and tracking

Apps that show ads use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the consent interface can present the purposes and vendors required for advertising. Depending on consent and technical eligibility, Google can serve personalized, non-personalized, limited or technical ads; refusing personalization does not mean that no technical processing occurs. On iOS apps that request tracking, ATT is a separate Apple permission. Denying ATT prevents access to IDFA for cross-app/site tracking. Magnet Grid currently does not request ATT.

Firebase Analytics uses pseudonymous identifiers and usage events. Advertising consent must not be understood as a blanket consent to all analytics. Consent and analytics-storage configuration must follow the applicable Google/Firebase configuration and law.
