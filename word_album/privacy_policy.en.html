1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Word Album app handles user data. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here. If you disagree with any part of it, please do not use the app.

2. What data we collect

The app does not ask for or directly collect personally identifiable information (name, email address, phone number). We handle the following game data:

  • List of solved puzzles
  • Play-streak counter
  • Hint balance and coin balance
  • Daily task progress and statistics (e.g. best time, solve time)
  • Saved game states
  • Settings (theme, language, daily reminder status)

This data is primarily stored locally on your device (shared preferences).

Cloud backup

The app also creates an optional, automatic cloud backup of the game data listed above, using Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore), so a fresh install or a new device doesn't lose your progress. For this, the app creates an anonymous Firebase identifier on your device — we do not request or attach a name, email address, phone number, or login credential to it. The backup covers only the game data listed above, and is restored from the cloud only when the device has no local progress yet (e.g. a fresh install).

4. Advertising and tracking

On first launch — for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland — the app shows Google's UMP consent form, where you can choose whether to allow personalized ads. Until you give consent, the app can only request non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time via Settings → "Manage Consent" (where this option is available in your region).

On iOS, we separately request permission via the system's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) mechanism before using your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for more personalized ads. You can revoke this at any time in Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

See the Manage Consent document for details.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed specifically at children; it is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request or collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address below and we will act promptly to delete it.

6. Data security and retention

The security of data stored on your device depends on your device's own security settings. Data stored in the cloud (backup) is protected by Google Firebase's industry-standard infrastructure. We retain game data for as long as you use the app, or until you request its deletion.

7. Your rights

GDPR / UK GDPR (EEA, UK users): You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, port, and object to processing of data concerning you. Since the app does not directly collect identifiable personal data, these rights primarily apply to the anonymous game data stored in the cloud — you can trigger its deletion by uninstalling the app, or by contacting us at the email address below.

CCPA/CPRA (California): We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond what is described in this policy (the operation of the advertising SDK). California residents have the right to know about and delete their data.

8. International data transfers

Google Firebase's infrastructure operates globally across multiple data centers, so cloud backup data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers are covered by Google's contractual safeguards (e.g. EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

9. Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the "last updated" date at the top. We recommend checking this page periodically.
