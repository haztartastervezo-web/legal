Privacy Policy

Clusters (com.enila.icongroups) · Last updated: September 3, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the game Clusters (com.enila.icongroups) (“the app”, “we”) handles information when you use it. The app is made by an independent developer based in the European Union. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Data controller

The data controller and provider of the app is Gábor Enikő, 1123 Budapest, Nagyenyed utca 5., Hungary.

Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device using standard app storage and is not sent to us:

  • Game progress, unlocked levels and best scores
  • App settings (language, sound, theme, daily reminder)
  • Your advertising consent choices

Data processed in the cloud (optional)

If cloud backup is available and enabled, an anonymous account is created through Firebase Authentication (a random identifier, with no name or email). Your game progress is then stored in Google Cloud Firestore so it can be restored on a new device. On Android you may optionally link Google Play Games, and on iOS Game Center, to keep progress across devices.

  • An anonymous user identifier
  • Game progress and statistics
  • When you send feedback: your message plus device model, operating system version, platform, app version, language and a timestamp

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including rewarded and banner ads. For this, Google may process device and advertising identifiers. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland a Google-certified consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads load, and you can choose personalized or non-personalized ads.

On iOS, the system App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the app may use identifiers for cross-app tracking. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. See the Manage Consent page for details.

Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate usage (for example which screens are opened and how sessions progress) so we can improve the game. This data is tied to a randomly generated app-instance identifier, not to your identity.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, a local notification is scheduled on your device. It is generated entirely on the device and no data is sent to us for this purpose.

Legal bases for processing

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for personalized advertising and, on iOS, for the use of identifiers for tracking; you can withdraw it at any time
  • Provision of a service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)) — to run the game and, when you use them, optional cloud backup and feedback handling
  • Our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — to show non-personalized advertising that funds the app, to keep it secure and stable, and to understand aggregate usage; you may object to this processing

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data, including:

  • Access, correction and deletion
  • Data portability, and objection to or restriction of processing
  • Withdrawing consent at any time
  • Lodging a complaint with your data protection authority

In Hungary, the competent supervisory authority is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, naih.hu); you may also contact the authority in your own country of residence.

To exercise these rights, or to have anonymous cloud data deleted, contact us at the address below. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for money or for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the ad choices described above, and we honor recognized opt-out preference signals where applicable.

Data retention

Local data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Cloud backup data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Feedback messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your request.

International transfers

Google and Apple may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.
