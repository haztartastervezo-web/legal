Privacy Policy

BeeFlow · Last updated: September 25, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BeeFlow (“the App”, “we”) handles information when you use it. BeeFlow is a color-flow hex puzzle game developed by Enila Studio, an independent developer based in the European Union.

Data controller

The data controller and provider of the App is Gábor Enikő, 1123 Budapest, Nagyenyed utca 5., Hungary.

Data stored on your device

BeeFlow stores information required for the game locally on your device. This may include:

  • game progress and completed levels;
  • in-game progress, rewards and unlocks;
  • game settings and preferences.

This information is stored using local app storage and is not sent to us. It is not intended to identify you personally.

Data sent to us

The current version of BeeFlow does not create user accounts and does not send your game progress or settings to Enila Studio servers.

BeeFlow does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, cloud-backup, or account-sign-in services within the App.

Advertising and tracking

The current version of BeeFlow does not display third-party advertising and does not use advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.

If advertising, analytics, cloud backup, or other services that process user data are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those features are introduced.

App stores and platform services

If you download BeeFlow from an app store, the store operator (for example Apple or Google) may process information about downloads, purchases, device or account activity under its own privacy policy. That processing is controlled by the relevant platform provider and is separate from the data handling described in this Privacy Policy.

Legal basis for processing

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, local processing necessary to operate the App is carried out to provide the service you request. Because the current version does not transmit game data to us, Enila Studio does not maintain a server-side profile of BeeFlow users.

Data retention

Locally stored BeeFlow data remains on your device until you clear the App's data, reset the relevant game data where such an option is available, or uninstall the App. Enila Studio does not retain a server-side copy of this local game data.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and data portability. Because the current version of BeeFlow does not maintain user accounts or transmit your local game data to Enila Studio, we generally do not hold personal game data that can be retrieved or deleted from our servers.

If you contact us directly, we will process the information contained in your message only as necessary to respond to your request.

In Hungary, the competent supervisory authority is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, naih.hu). You may also contact the data protection authority in your country of residence.

Sale or sharing of personal information

Enila Studio does not sell personal information. The current version of BeeFlow does not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Children's privacy

BeeFlow is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current version of the App. If you believe that a child has sent personal information to us directly, please contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

International data transfers

The current version of BeeFlow does not transmit your game progress or settings to Enila Studio servers. App-store and operating-system providers may process information internationally under their own privacy policies and safeguards.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when BeeFlow changes or when legal requirements change. The current version will always be published at this address, and the “Last updated” date above will show when it was revised.
