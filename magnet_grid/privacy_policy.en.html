Privacy Policy

App: Magnet Grid (com.enila.magnetgrid) · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Controller and provider: Fütyü-Gábor Enikő (Enila Studio), private individual · 4032 Debrecen, Cívis utca 3., Hungary · E-mail: haztartastervezo@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Magnet Grid mobile app (the “App”), a logic puzzle game played on a grid, processes, for what purposes, with whom it is shared and what rights you have. It is for information only: where your consent is required, the App asks for it separately.

In short: Your game progress and settings are stored on your device. So that your progress is not lost, the App also automatically backs it up to Google Firebase under a random, pseudonymous ID — without your name, e-mail address or password. The App shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google Analytics for Firebase for usage statistics. These Google services process pseudonymous technical identifiers (e.g. app-instance ID or advertising ID) and device data — this is not anonymous data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your ad choices; on iOS, Apple's tracking permission prompt is also shown.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Game progress: completed puzzles and levels, statistics, streaks and the state of an unfinished game
  • In-game coins, hints and other items earned in the game (these have no monetary value)
  • Settings (e.g. language, theme, sound, vibration)
  • Which of our other apps the “More apps” card recommended last

This data stays on your device, except for the backup described in section 3.

Uninstalling the App deletes this local data from your device. Note: if you use your device's own backup (e.g. Android or iCloud device backup), the operating system may also back up app data according to your device settings — this is outside our control.

3. Automatic cloud backup (Google Firebase)

When you first open the App, it automatically creates a pseudonymous account for you with Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in). This account has a random user ID (UID) and is not linked to your name, e-mail address or phone number.

Under this UID the App automatically saves a copy of your game progress and settings (see section 2) in Google Cloud Firestore — usually when you leave the App — so that your progress is protected and can be restored where possible. This backup is part of how the App works and contains no contact details.

If you wish, you can generate a short restore code in the App. The code is stored in Firestore together with a reference to your backup and lets you restore your progress on another device. Anyone who knows the code can restore the backup, so keep it to yourself.

Uninstalling the App does not by itself delete the cloud backup. To have it deleted, write to us (section 1). Because the account is pseudonymous, we need information to find your backup: your restore code if you have one, otherwise the approximate date of last use and your device model.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and funded by advertising: banner ads, native ads that blend into the screen, full-screen (interstitial) ads shown occasionally between levels and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To show, limit, measure and — where permitted — personalise ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process: the advertising ID (Android) and, on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking (ATT); your IP address and the approximate location derived from it; device and app data (model, OS version, language, app version); and ad interactions (impressions, clicks). Google acts under its own rules: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the App is used and to improve its puzzles and features, the App sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase — for example app opens, starting and completing puzzles or levels, hint use, rewarded-ad interactions and impressions and clicks of the “More apps” card. Firebase also collects data automatically, such as a randomly generated app-instance ID, device model, OS version, app version, language/region, approximate location derived from the IP address and session data; on Android it may also receive the advertising ID.

These identifiers are pseudonymous: we do not link them to your name or contact details, but under the GDPR they are personal data. Analytics starts when the App is opened and is not controlled by the ad consent form. You can object to analytics processing at any time by writing to us (section 1).

6. Consent and tracking choices

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: at start-up the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) — a consent management platform certified by Google — to ask for your choices about storing/accessing information on your device and about ad personalisation. The App only requests ads after this step. Depending on your choices, Google may show personalised, non-personalised or limited ads, or no ads at all. You can change your choice at any time in the App's settings under “Manage consent” / privacy settings (shown where Google requires it).
  • iOS – App Tracking Transparency (ATT): after the consent step, iOS asks whether the App may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the IDFA is not available to the App or to Google; ads may still be shown. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. ATT is independent of Google's consent form — both apply.

Details: Manage Consent.

7. Other functions and permissions

  • Contact by e-mail: if you write to us (e.g. via “Contact” or “I have an idea”), we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to reply.
  • Sharing and store links: “Share” opens your device's share sheet; “Rate us” and the “More apps” card open the app store. We receive no data about these actions beyond the analytics events described above.
  • In-app update (Android): the App asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. This is handled by Google Play, not by our servers.

Permissions the App uses: internet access. The App does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files, and has no in-app purchases.
