Manage Consent — MagnetGrid

Last updated: September 30, 2026

MagnetGrid uses Google AdMob to display advertising. This page explains how advertising-related privacy choices are handled in the App.

1. Google UMP Privacy Messages

MagnetGrid uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When the App starts, UMP requests updated consent information and determines whether a privacy message is required based on the user's region and the privacy messages configured for the App.

If a consent form is required, UMP may ask you to make choices related to personalized advertising, advertising purposes, vendors, or other privacy options supported by Google for your region.

2. How to Change Your Choice

If Google UMP reports that a privacy-options entry point is required, MagnetGrid shows Settings → Manage consent. You can use that option to reopen Google's privacy-options form and change the choices available to you.

The exact controls and choices shown in the form are provided by Google and may vary by region, applicable law, and the privacy-message configuration used by the App.

3. What Happens If You Do Not Consent to Personalized Advertising

The core game remains usable. Depending on the consent state and Google's supported ad-serving mode, advertising may be non-personalized, limited, technically served without personalization, or unavailable. Optional rewarded benefits require an eligible ad to be available.

4. App Tracking Transparency on iOS

MagnetGrid does not currently request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. The App therefore does not rely on ATT-authorized access to the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. Third-party services may still process data that Apple's rules allow without ATT authorization.

5. Analytics and Cloud Sync

Advertising consent managed through UMP is separate from the App's Firebase Analytics and automatic anonymous Firebase cloud-sync features. For information about those services and the data they process, see the Privacy Policy.
