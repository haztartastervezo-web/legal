Manage Consent — MagnetGrid

Last updated: August 26, 2026

MagnetGrid shows ads through Google AdMob. This page explains what consent choices MagnetGrid asks for, and how you can change them.

1. What Consent We Ask For

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, MagnetGrid shows a consent form (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform, UMP) the first time you open the App, asking whether you agree to personalized advertising. Users outside these regions are not shown this form, since it is only legally required there.

MagnetGrid does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission on iOS and does not use Firebase Crashlytics or any other tracking consent beyond the advertising consent described above.

2. How to Change Your Choice

You can revisit and change your advertising consent at any time:

  • In the App: open Settings → "Manage consent" to reopen the consent form and update your choice. This option is shown only to users in regions where Google's consent requirements apply.
  • On iOS (if a tracking permission is ever added in a future version): Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. What Happens If You Decline

If you do not consent to personalized advertising, MagnetGrid continues to work exactly the same way — you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized rather than tailored to your interests.

4. EEA/UK/Switzerland Users

Google's UMP consent flow reflects the requirements of the GDPR/UK GDPR and Google's EU User Consent Policy for users in these regions, including the ability to withdraw consent as easily as it was given.
