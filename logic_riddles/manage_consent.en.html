Manage Consent

App: Logic Riddles (com.enila.logicriddles) · Developer: Enila Studio · Last updated: October 10, 2026

This page describes the consent and permission choices that actually exist in Logic Riddles (the "App"), what each one controls, and how to change it. For full details on data processing, see the Privacy Policy.

1. Ad consent – Google User Messaging Platform (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

What it controls: whether Google AdMob and the ad partners listed in the dialog may store or access information on your device (such as the advertising ID) and whether ads may be personalized.

When it appears: on startup, if you are in a region where Google requires consent. Ads are requested only after this step.

If you decline or limit consent: the game remains fully playable. Depending on your choices, Google may show non-personalized ads, limited ads, or no ads. If no ad is available, ad-based extras (e.g. a rewarded hint or ad-free period) may be temporarily unavailable.

How to change it: open Settings → "Manage consent" in the App. This reopens Google's privacy options form where you can change or withdraw your consent at any time. The menu item appears where Google's rules require it (EEA/UK/Switzerland). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

2. Tracking permission – Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS only)

What it controls: whether the App may use the IDFA (Apple's advertising identifier) to track you across other companies' apps and websites, e.g. for personalized ads.

When it appears: once, after the Google consent step, before ads are initialized.

If you decline: the IDFA is not made available to the App or to Google. Ads may still be shown. Declining ATT is separate from the Google consent choice — both apply.

How to change it: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (or Settings → Logic Riddles).

3. Advertising ID on Android

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).

4. Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Usage analytics is not controlled by the ad-consent dialog and there is currently no in-app switch for it. It is based on our legitimate interest (see the Privacy Policy). If you want to object to analytics processing, email us at haztartastervezo@gmail.com.

5. Device permissions

The App requests only internet access. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or notification permission.
