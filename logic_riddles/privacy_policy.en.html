Privacy Policy

App: Logic Riddles (Android package / iOS bundle ID: com.enila.logicriddles) · Developer: Enila Studio · Last updated: October 10, 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the Logic Riddles mobile app (the "App"), a visual logic-grid puzzle game, processes, why, with whom it is shared, and what rights you have. This policy is for information only; where your consent is required, the App asks for it separately.

In short: Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. The App has no user accounts, does not ask for your name, email address or password, and does not upload your game data to our own servers. The App shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google Analytics for Firebase for usage statistics. These Google services process pseudonymous technical identifiers (for example an app-instance ID or advertising ID) and device data — this is not anonymous data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent dialog asks for your ad choices; on iOS, Apple's tracking permission prompt is shown separately.

1. Controller and contact

The controller is Enila Studio, an independent app developer based in the European Union. For any privacy question or request, write to: haztartastervezo@gmail.com.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Puzzle progress: completed levels and chapters, daily challenge status, an unfinished puzzle's board and elapsed time, tutorials already seen
  • Settings: language, theme, sound, vibration, left-handed mode, board zoom and lock
  • The expiry time of a temporary ad-free period earned by watching a rewarded ad
  • Which of our other apps was last recommended to you in the in-app "our other apps" card

This data is not sent to us. You can delete your progress with Settings → "Reset progress"; uninstalling the App removes all of this local data from your device. Note: if you use your device's own backup (e.g. Android or iCloud device backup), the operating system may include app data in that backup under your device settings — this is outside our control.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and funded by ads: banner ads, occasional interstitial ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads (e.g. for hints or an ad-free period) that you choose to watch. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To deliver, limit, measure and — if permitted — personalize ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process: the advertising ID (Android) or, only with your ATT permission, the IDFA (iOS); IP address and approximate location derived from it; device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version); and ad interactions (impressions, clicks). Google acts under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The App also records in its analytics when an interstitial ad is shown, clicked or closed (with the level number).

4. Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the App is used and to improve puzzles and features, the App sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase, for example: app opened, settings opened, puzzle started/completed (puzzle ID, level, mode), hint used, rewarded hint or move used, interstitial ad shown/clicked/closed, and impressions/clicks of the "our other apps" recommendation card (which app was shown, where, and the platform). Firebase also collects automatically recorded data such as a randomly generated app-instance ID, device model, OS version, app version, language/region, approximate location (derived from the IP address) and session information; on Android it may also receive the advertising ID.

These identifiers are pseudonymous: we do not link them to your name or contact details, but they are personal data under the GDPR. Analytics collection starts when the App is opened and is not controlled by the ad-consent dialog. If you object to analytics processing, contact us (section 1).

5. Consent and tracking choices

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: on startup the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform, to ask for your choices about storing/accessing information on your device and ad personalization. Ads are only requested after this step. Depending on your choices, Google may show personalized ads, non-personalized ads, limited ads, or no ads at all. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → "Manage consent" (shown where Google requires it).
  • iOS – App Tracking Transparency (ATT): after the consent step, iOS asks whether the App may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the IDFA is not made available to the App or Google; ads may still be shown. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. ATT is separate from the Google consent dialog — both apply.

Details: Manage Consent.

6. Other functions

  • Contact by email: if you write to us via "Contact support", we receive your email address and whatever you write; we use it only to answer you.
  • Share and store links: "Share" opens your device's system share sheet with a store link; "Rate us" and "our other apps" open the app store. We do not receive data about these actions beyond the analytics events in section 4.
  • In-app update (Android): the App asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. This is handled by Google Play, not our servers.

The App requests only internet access. It does not access your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, sends no push notifications, and contains no in-app purchases.

8. Recipients (processors and independent providers)

  • Google (Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase) — analytics, as our processor. Firebase privacy information
  • Google AdMob and, where applicable, ad partners displayed in the consent dialog — ad delivery; Google acts partly as an independent controller under its own terms.
  • Google Play (Android) — in-app update check.
  • Apple (iOS) — ATT permission handled by iOS.

We do not sell your personal data for money. We do not share data with any other third parties except where required by law.

9. Retention

  • Local data: until you reset your progress or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics data: retained by Google according to the data-retention setting of our Google Analytics property and Google's own retention policies; aggregated reports may be kept longer.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under Google's retention policies (policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted unless we must keep them by law.

Uninstalling the App deletes local data but does not by itself delete data already received by Google; see section 10.

10. Your rights and deletion

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interest (including analytics), and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Hungary: NAIH, naih.hu).

Because the App has no accounts, we usually cannot identify you from your email request alone. To help with analytics data, we may ask for the app-instance ID or other details. You can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings, withdraw ad consent via "Manage consent", and revoke tracking permission on iOS.

US residents: depending on your state (e.g. California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to know, access, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of "sale"/"sharing" or targeted advertising. Use of the advertising ID for personalized ads may count as "sharing" under some state laws; you can opt out by limiting ad tracking / deleting your advertising ID in device settings, declining tracking on iOS, or contacting us. We do not sell personal information for money.

11. International transfers

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (where Google is certified) and/or Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

12. Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game; it is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us personal data (e.g. by email), contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Local data is stored in the App's private storage on your device.

14. Automated decision-making

The App does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing. Ad selection by Google may be automated.

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. The current version is always published at this address, with the date at the top.
