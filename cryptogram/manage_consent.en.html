Manage Consent

App: Cryptogram / Kódfejtő · Last updated: September 16, 2026

This page supplements the Privacy Policy. It explains the App’s advertising/privacy-choice mechanisms and how you can change those choices.

1. Google advertising privacy choices

Where required by applicable law or Google policy, including for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent-management interface for advertising technologies.

The exact choices shown by Google can depend on your region, the advertising vendors involved and current legal/policy requirements. The App only requests ads when Google’s SDK reports that ads may be requested under the current consent/privacy state.

2. Personalized advertising

Where personalized advertising requires consent, it is requested only after the applicable Google consent flow permits it. If you decline personalization, the App may still show non-personalized or otherwise limited ads where allowed. Rewarded-ad gameplay features are not conditioned on agreeing to personalized advertising.

3. iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission is separate from Google’s consent interface. ATT controls whether the App may access tracking-related identifiers such as IDFA and participate in tracking across other companies’ apps or websites where applicable.

You can change this permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Refusing ATT does not disable the game; it limits tracking/personalization capabilities.

4. Reopening privacy options

If Google indicates that privacy options must be available in your region, the App provides an “Ad privacy” / privacy-options entry from the Profile/privacy area. Opening it displays Google’s available privacy choices again so you can review or change them.

5. Analytics

Firebase Analytics is described separately in the Privacy Policy. We do not describe Analytics as inherently anonymous. It can use pseudonymous app/device identifiers and technical event data. The legal basis and any consent requirement depend on the applicable privacy/ePrivacy rules and the SDK/storage behavior in the relevant jurisdiction.

6. Withdrawal of consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time using the available Google privacy options and, for iOS tracking, Apple’s system settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
