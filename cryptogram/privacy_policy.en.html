2. Data processed by the App

2.1 Local game and preference data

The App stores data on your device so gameplay can continue between sessions. Depending on the features you use, this may include puzzle state and progress, solved puzzles, streaks, statistics, unlocked content, coins, hints and other virtual rewards, challenge state, language, theme, sound, notification preferences and similar App settings.

Local data is not sent to us merely because it exists on your device. However, the cloud-sync feature described below synchronizes the App’s locally stored game/preference state to Firebase.

2.2 Pseudonymous Firebase account and cloud synchronization

After Firebase initializes, the App automatically creates or uses a Firebase Authentication account without asking for a name, email address or password. Firebase calls this an “anonymous” account, but for privacy-law purposes we treat its unique Firebase user ID (UID) as a pseudonymous online identifier, not as data that is necessarily anonymous.

The App uses Cloud Firestore to synchronize the locally stored game/preference state under that UID. On a fresh installation the App may restore cloud data where the relevant account can be identified; when the App goes to the background, changed local state may be uploaded. This is intended to protect game progress and support recovery.

You can also generate a 6-character restore code. A restore-code record is stored in Firestore and mapped to the relevant Firebase UID so that progress can be recovered on another installation. Anyone who obtains a valid restore code may be able to initiate restoration, so please keep the code private.

2.3 Feedback

If you voluntarily submit feedback, the App sends the rating, optional message, device model/manufacturer, operating-system version, platform, App version/build, selected locale and server-side submission time to Cloud Firestore. Do not include passwords, health information, financial information or other sensitive personal data in the free-text field.

2.4 Bug/content reports

If you voluntarily report an incorrect puzzle or other content issue, the App may send the puzzle, series and chapter identifiers, report type, optional free-text message, quote identifier where applicable, device model, platform, App version/build, selected locale and server-side submission time to Cloud Firestore.

2.5 Language requests

If you use the language-request feature, the requested language and any optional text you provide may be stored in Firestore together with technical context needed to process the request. A related analytics event may also record the requested language.

2.6 Firebase Analytics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and to improve gameplay. Events can include, for example, onboarding choices, puzzle/series/chapter identifiers, puzzle start/completion/abandonment, duration, difficulty, error counts, hints used, completed chapters/books/series, reward-ad placement, language/app-locale information, notification opens and cross-promotion impressions or clicks. Analytics services use pseudonymous app/device identifiers and technical information generated by the SDK. We do not send your name or email address as Analytics user properties.

2.7 Advertising and ad measurement

The App uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and may show banner, native and rewarded ads. Google and participating ad-technology providers may process advertising identifiers (where available and permitted), IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics and other technical information for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, measurement and, where you consent, personalization.

2.8 Notifications and device features

The App can send local notifications/reminders if the operating system grants notification permission. Notification scheduling preferences are stored locally and may form part of the synchronized App state. The App may also use vibration/haptics and, on Android, mechanisms that allow scheduled reminders to be restored after device restart. An optional home-screen widget is rendered on the device.

7. Retention

  • Local App data: retained on the device until you clear App data or uninstall the App, subject to any device/OS backup and restore functionality.
  • Cloud game state and Firebase UID: retained while needed to provide synchronization/recovery, until deletion is requested, or until the service is discontinued and the data is no longer needed.
  • Restore-code mapping: retained while the related cloud save remains available, unless deletion is requested earlier.
  • Feedback and bug reports: normally deleted within 3 months of submission, unless a specific report must be retained longer to establish, exercise or defend legal claims or to investigate a security/abuse incident.
  • Language requests: normally retained for no longer than 3 months in identifiable/pseudonymous form; aggregate planning information may be retained without the free-text submission.
  • Firebase Analytics: user/event-level retention is determined by the retention settings of the relevant Firebase/Google Analytics property and Google’s service rules; aggregated reports may be retained longer. We use retention settings and aggregation appropriate to product analytics and do not retain identifiable data longer than necessary for the stated purpose.
