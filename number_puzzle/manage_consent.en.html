Manage Consent

App: SzámKirakó / Number Puzzle · Last updated: August 7, 2026

This page complements the Privacy Policy and summarizes what consent the App requests and how you can change it at any time.

1. What consent we request

  • Personalized advertising (EEA / UK / Switzerland): on startup, the App shows Google's standard consent dialog through its certified consent management platform (UMP), if required for your region.
  • Tracking permission (iOS only): Apple's native App Tracking Transparency (ATT) system prompt asks whether you allow the App and its ad partners to track your activity across other apps/websites using a device identifier (IDFA).

The App's usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) are not tied to your identity, so we do not request separate consent for them — this data cannot be used to identify you individually.

2. How to change your choice

Ad consent (UMP): tap "Ad privacy" (Privacy Options) in the Settings screen to reopen Google's consent dialog and change your choice at any time.

ATT tracking (iOS): you can allow or revoke the App's tracking permission at any time from iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, without reinstalling the App.

3. What happens if you decline

All App functionality — including rewards from watching a rewarded ad — remains available even if you decline consent. In that case, the App shows non-personalized ads, selected using contextual information rather than a device identifier.

4. Special rights for EEA/UK users

Under Google's EU User Consent Policy, users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland must give consent through a certified consent management platform (CMP) before personalized ads are served. This consent can be withdrawn at any time as described in section 2 above, and withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.
