Privacy Policy

App: SzámKirakó / Number Puzzle (package: com.puzzles.number_puzzle) · Last updated: September 4, 2026

This privacy policy explains how SzámKirakó (the "App") handles user data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: Your gameplay data is mainly stored on your device. The App also shows ads (Google AdMob), collects anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), and offers an anonymous cloud backup and an in-app feedback form (Firebase). The App never asks for your name, email, or a password, and it contains no purchases.

1. Data controller

The App is made by an independent developer. For privacy questions, contact: haztartastervezo@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Local data (stored only on your device)

  • Game state, saved board, and data needed to continue a game
  • Best scores, statistics, level and progress data
  • Unlocked rewards, in-game coins, daily bonus/chest status
  • App settings (e.g. sound, theme, difficulty, language, notification preferences)

2.2 Anonymous cloud backup (Firebase Authentication + Cloud Firestore)

The App lets you back up your progress anonymously to the cloud. This creates an anonymous Firebase identifier on your device that is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal identifier.

2.3 Feedback (Cloud Firestore)

If you submit feedback from the Profile screen (a rating and an optional message), the following is sent to our server: the rating, the message text, your device model and manufacturer, OS version, App version, your selected language, and the submission timestamp. This does not include your name or email unless you choose to include it in the message text yourself.

2.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its ad partners may use a device identifier (e.g. advertising ID / IDFA) and other technical data to serve and measure ads. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, we request consent for this as described in section 4.

2.5 Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Anonymous, aggregated gameplay events are recorded (e.g. level started/completed/failed, chapter completed, practice mode used, hint/shuffle used, feedback rating). These do not include personally identifiable information.

4. Ads and tracking (consent)

For users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, the App requests consent for personalized ads on startup via Google's certified consent management platform (User Messaging Platform, UMP). On iOS, the native Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is also shown before any device-identifier-based (personalized) ad is requested. If you decline, the App still works fully and shows non-personalized ads instead. You can change your choice at any time from the "Ad privacy" option in Settings. Consenting to personalized ads is entirely separate from — and not required for — using the App's core gameplay features, including rewarded ads. See Manage Consent for details.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided us with personal data, please contact us using the details in section 10 so we can remove it.

6. Data security and retention

Local data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App. Data sent to the cloud (backup, feedback) is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure with industry-standard access controls (Firestore security rules restrict backup data to the device holding the matching anonymous identifier). We retain this data only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, or until you request its deletion; on request, we will confirm once the deletion has been carried out.

7. Your rights

Users in the EEA/UK have rights under the GDPR to access, correct, delete, port, and object to processing of their data. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA to access and delete their data, and to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information — the App does not sell or share user data with third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration.

  • Local data: uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local data from your device.
  • Cloud data (backup, feedback): you can request its deletion, access, or correction using the email in section 10; we will act on your request within a reasonable time.

8. International data transfers

Our cloud services (Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics) are operated by Google LLC, which may transfer data outside the European Economic Area under Google's GDPR-compliant contractual safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time as the App's features change. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page. Please check back periodically.
