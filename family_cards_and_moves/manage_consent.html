This page explains the privacy choices available in Family Cards & Moves. It does not collect consent or change settings remotely.

Advertising and tracking

This version has no ad network, tracking-based advertising, remote analytics or advertising identifier use. It has no Google UMP advertising-consent form. There is no tracking request to accept. Recommendations for our other apps are store links, not personalised network ads.

Local reminders

Notification permission is requested when you enable a routine reminder. You can decline, turn reminders off in the app, or revoke permission through device settings. Other features remain usable. Check lock-screen previews because reminders may display routine names.

Selected files

You may cancel the system picker without choosing an image, audio file or backup. A selected image or song is copied into app storage. Deleting the original outside the app does not delete the imported copy. Remove goal images or selected music through the relevant editor where available, use “Start over” for the family setup, or clear app storage for a full local reset.

Sharing and backups

“Save my setup” opens the system share sheet with a readable JSON file. Check the destination before sharing. Manage any device or cloud backups separately. We cannot recall a file sent to another service.

Parent PIN and other preferences

Set, change or remove the optional PIN in the parent-lock settings. Language, appearance and the PIN survive “Start over”. The PIN is a convenience feature, not verified parental consent or encrypted storage.

Rights and contact

See Data Deletion for a complete reset. For support correspondence or privacy rights, contact haztartastervezo@gmail.com. Accepting app terms is not blanket consent to personal-data processing. If future features require consent, the app must request it separately and provide a way to withdraw it.
