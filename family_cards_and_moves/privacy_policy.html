This policy explains how Family Cards & Moves handles information in its family routines, visual cards, everyday support KITs and movement activities. It covers the mobile app and these legal pages. Parents and caregivers should read it before setting up the app for a child.

The app stores family content on the device. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives your routines, pictures, music or progress. This version has no third-party advertising, remote analytics or developer-operated crash reporting. Optional exports, device backups, support emails and external services are explained below.

The provider and data controller for processing under our control is Fütyü-Gábor Enikő (Enila Studio), private individual · 4032 Debrecen, Cívis utca 3., Hungary · haztartastervezo@gmail.com. “Enila Studio” is a publishing name. Contact us using the email above for privacy questions and rights requests.

2. Information handled on your device

  • Routine names, steps, custom card labels, emojis, descriptions, movement selections and recorded best completion times.
  • Favourite KITs, daily selections, feedback ratings, practice items and notes.
  • School timetable entries; weekly goals, progress and optional images selected for goals.
  • Daily completion history and sun points. The saved daily history is limited to approximately 60 days.
  • Profile mode, music choices, copies of imported audio, reminder times, language, appearance and the optional parent PIN.
  • Local review-prompt counters, such as active days, foreground time and prompt cooldowns.

Custom text or images can contain personal information, including information about a child or health. Use a nickname where possible and avoid unnecessary diagnoses, medical details, addresses or school identifiers. The app does not require a child's real name, date of birth or account credentials. Its profile selection is a usage mode, not an online identity.

3. Files, notifications and permissions

When you select a goal image or audio file, the system picker provides the selected file and the app copies it into app storage. A picker may also offer files from a cloud provider connected to your device. The app does not upload the imported file to us. It does not use a camera or microphone recording feature, location or contacts.

Optional routine reminders are scheduled locally after notification permission is requested when you enable a reminder. They are not server-sent push messages. A routine's name may appear on your lock screen; adjust notification previews if this is sensitive. Declining notifications does not prevent using routines.

4. Manual exports and operating-system backups

“Save my setup” creates a readable JSON backup and opens the device share sheet. The export includes routines, custom card definitions, favourites, ratings, practice items, timetable, weekly goal definitions, profile/music settings and reminder settings. It excludes daily progress, imported audio files, goal image files and the parent PIN. Language and appearance preferences are stored separately and are not part of this export. The export is not encrypted by the app.

You choose where to send or save it. Anyone with access to that file may read it. A selected mail, storage or messaging provider processes it under its own terms. The app may retain a temporary export file in its cache until it is removed by the operating system or app storage is cleared.

Google, Apple or computer/device backup services may separately back up app data and files, depending on the platform, build configuration and your settings. These are different from the manual export and may contain additional data. We do not administer these backups. Delete unwanted copies through the provider or device settings; a later restore may bring data back.

5. External services and support

  • Google Play: Android update checks and installations use Google Play. Ratings and other-app links open the relevant store. Google or Apple may process device, installation, review and diagnostic information under their own policies.
  • Other-app recommendations: the app contains links promoting our other apps. These are not personalised by an advertising network. Each linked app has its own terms and privacy practices.
  • Support email: if you send a message, we receive your email address, message and any attachments or technical information you include. Our support mailbox uses Google Gmail. Please have an adult contact us and omit unnecessary information about children.
  • Legal pages: opening these pages makes a request to GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. These pages contain no developer-added tracking scripts, embedded advertising, analytics, forms or cookies.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy; Apple Privacy Policy; GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Purposes and legal bases

Local app processing enables the routines, preferences and optional functions requested by the adult user. Where GDPR applies to that processing under our control, we rely on Article 6(1)(b) for the requested service. Optional notification permission and file selection are device controls; they are not blanket consent to other processing. We do not rely on a child's acceptance of these documents as parental consent or as a basis for collecting health information.

For support, Article 6(1)(b) applies to requests necessary to provide the service; for general correspondence we rely on Article 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in responding and resolving problems. We rely on Article 6(1)(c) for legally required retention or disclosures, and Article 6(1)(f) for proportionate handling of legal claims. Providing support information is optional, but without the necessary details we may not be able to answer your request. We do not request special-category health data in support.

7. Recipients and international processing

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising or send local family content to an analytics provider. Support correspondence is handled in our Gmail mailbox and may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal claims. User-selected export destinations and platform services process the information described above.

Google, Apple, GitHub and a provider you choose may process information outside the EEA, including in the United States. Their applicable transfer safeguards and notices are described in their privacy policies; where required, safeguards may include an applicable adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about transfers involving support correspondence under our control.

8. Retention and deletion

Local setup is kept until changed or deleted. “Start over” removes the main family setup and attempts to delete imported songs and goal images, then restores built-in routines. It keeps the parent PIN, language, appearance and review-prompt preferences. A recovery copy of an unreadable save and temporary export files may also remain. For a complete local reset, clear app storage or remove the app and its data through device settings. External backups and files must be deleted separately. See Data Deletion for steps.

We retain support emails while needed to resolve the request and relevant follow-up, then delete them unless a legal obligation or a specific unresolved claim requires retention. Retained material is limited to that purpose. Mail-provider backups may persist according to the provider's retention processes.

9. Children and parental choices

The app is intended for family use, including by children with adult involvement. Its routines and movement features do not require children to send us information. There is no child account, public profile, chat or public sharing feed. Adults should manage file imports, exports, external links and support contacts. The optional parent PIN is a convenience barrier; it is not verified parental consent or strong security. If a child sends information to support, contact us so we can remove unnecessary information. See Children's Privacy and Parent Information.

10. Security

Local data is stored in app preferences and app files, relying on operating-system protections. The app does not add database encryption or encrypt its JSON exports. The parent PIN is stored as a local preference and is not a substitute for a device passcode. Keep the device secured, current and under adult control. No storage or communications system can be guaranteed completely secure.
