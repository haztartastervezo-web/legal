Privacy Policy

App: Family Cards & Moves (com.enila.cardsmoves) · Last updated: October 14, 2026

Controller and provider: Fütyü-Gábor Enikő (Enila Studio), private individual · 4032 Debrecen, Cívis utca 3., Hungary · E-mail: haztartastervezo@gmail.com

This privacy policy explains what data the Family Cards & Moves mobile app (the "App") — picture routines, step-by-step KITs and playful movements for families, including families with ADHD — processes, and what rights you have. This policy is for information only; using the App does not mean you consent to anything. The App does not ask for consent because it does not need any.

In short: Everything you create or record in the App — routines, own pictures, notes, ratings, daily progress and settings — is stored only on your device. We do not receive it. The App has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDK, and the Android version does not even have permission to access the internet. It does not use any advertising identifier.

1. Controller and contact

The controller of your personal data and the provider of the App is Fütyü-Gábor Enikő, private individual, publishing apps under the name Enila Studio (address: 4032 Debrecen, Cívis utca 3., Hungary). For any privacy question or request, write to haztartastervezo@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as we are not required to.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Picture routines: their names, icons and steps, built-in routines you changed, and your best "race" times
  • Your own picture cards and changes to built-in cards (name, emoji, colour, short description)
  • KITs: favourites, which KITs are part of the daily plan, and your rating at the end of a KIT ("It helped" / "Partly" / "Not this time")
  • Daily sun: which routines and KITs were completed on which day and the points collected (kept for about 60 days)
  • The "keep practising" list, including notes you type in yourself
  • Profile choice (child / parent / together)
  • Background music: whether it is on, and — if you choose your own music file — a copy of that file in the App's private storage
  • Settings: app language
  • The state of the "rate the app" prompt (e.g. time spent in the App, active days, when it may be shown again)

Some of this information — for example routines, practice notes or the "Medicine / vitamin" picture — can relate to a child's everyday life or health. It is stored only in the App's private storage on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else; we have no access to it. Please do not enter more about your child in free-text notes than you need.

Device backups: if you use your device's own backup (Android backup to your Google account, iCloud or computer backups on iOS), the operating system may include the App's data in that backup according to your device settings. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under your account with them, not by us.

3. Functions that open other apps or services

  • Contact and feedback by email: "Contact / support" and the "I have an idea" answer of the rate prompt open your email app. If you send an email, we receive your email address and what you write, and use it only to answer you.
  • Share, rate and our other apps: "Share" opens your device's share sheet with a store link; "Rate us", the rate prompt and "Our other apps" open the app store; the legal documents open in your browser. We receive no data about these actions; the store or browser handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • Own music file: when you choose a music file, your device's file picker opens; only the file you pick is copied into the App's private storage. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • In-app update (Android): the App asks the Google Play app on your device whether a newer version is available. This is handled by Google Play under its own privacy policy, not by our servers.

4. What the App does not do

The App does not create accounts, does not ask for your or your child's name, age, email address or password, does not show ads, does not use analytics, crash reporting or tracking, does not access the advertising ID (AAID/IDFA), location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos, and does not send push notifications. Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is not shown because the App does not track you.

6. Recipients

We do not share, sell or rent personal data. The only personal data we can receive at all are emails you choose to send us, which are handled in our email service (Google Gmail, provider: Google). The Google Play in-app update check, the app stores, your browser, your email app and your device's backup are run by their providers under their own privacy policies. Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, relying on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (under which Google is certified) and/or the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: until you delete it in the App, use Settings → "Start over" (deletes all routines, own pictures, own music files, favourites, ratings, practice list and sun points and restores the built-in routines; the app language is kept), clear the App's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes the App's data from the device; copies in your own device backups are kept according to your backup settings.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted unless the law requires us to keep them.

8. Your rights

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and the right to object. Because the data in the App never reaches us, you can exercise these directly on your device (view, change or delete it in the App). For support emails, write to us. You can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the country of your residence. In Hungary: Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11.; postal address: 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9.; phone: +36 1 391 1400; naih.hu. US residents: we do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

9. Children

The App is designed for families to use together: parents set it up, and children use the routines, KITs and movements, ideally with an adult. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — the App collects no personal information at all and sends nothing to us. Links that leave the App (store, email, browser) are in Settings, which is meant for parents; the rate prompt and "Our other apps" also open the app store. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is kept in the App's private storage, which the operating system protects from other apps. Keep your device locked and up to date; anyone who can unlock the device can see the App's data.

11. Automated decisions

The App does not make decisions based solely on automated processing or profiling with legal or similarly significant effects.

12. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change (for example if a future version adds ads, analytics or cloud backup), we will update this policy before the change and change the "Last updated" date; where consent is required, the App will ask for it separately.
