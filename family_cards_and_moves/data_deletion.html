Data Deletion

Routine Cards & Moves · Android package: com.enila.cardsmoves
Last updated: October 19, 2026

Provider: Fütyü-Gábor Enikő (Enila Studio), private individual · 4032 Debrecen, Cívis utca 3., Hungary · haztartastervezo@gmail.com

Routine Cards & Moves has no online user account and no developer-hosted database of family content. There is therefore no online account to close. This page explains how to remove local data and request deletion of support correspondence.

1. Reset your family setup

Open the Parent area, then Settings, choose “Start over”, and confirm. Unlock the parent area first if a PIN is enabled. This resets routines, custom cards, favourites, ratings, routine feedback, practice items, timetable, weekly goals, daily progress, paused-routine state and reminder settings, and restores built-in defaults. It also attempts to remove imported songs and goal images. File deletion can fail because of device errors.

This is not a complete wipe: the parent PIN, language, appearance, colour palette, dismissed tips, review-prompt preferences, separately stored movement favourites/feedback/custom sequences and the daily parent-key entitlement remain. A saved recovery copy of previously unreadable data and temporary exported files may remain. Check scheduled notifications and disable notification permission if needed.

2. Remove all local app data

  • Android: open device Settings → Apps → Routine Cards & Moves → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage / Clear data. Menu names vary. Clearing cache alone does not erase all app data.
  • iPhone or iPad, if installed: open Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → Routine Cards & Moves → Delete App. Choose deletion rather than “Offload App”, which keeps documents and data.

Removing an app and its data generally removes its local private files. Review platform backup settings before reinstalling, as restoration may bring data back. No method here promises forensic erasure from a device or provider backup.

3. Remove exported and backed-up copies

Delete JSON setup files from Files/Downloads, cloud drives, sent messages, email attachments, other recipients and recycle bins as appropriate. Delete device backups or app data in those backups using the relevant provider's controls. Clearing app storage does not delete a source image or audio file, or copies you shared elsewhere.

4. Ask us to delete support correspondence

Email haztartastervezo@gmail.com with the subject “Routine Cards & Moves – data deletion”. Prefer the address you used to contact us. Do not send a child's photo, identity document, PIN or backup file. We may request minimal information needed to identify the correspondence and verify your request.

For GDPR requests, we normally respond within one month. Where the law allows an extension, we will explain it. We delete information we no longer need, subject to legal obligations and necessary retention for specific claims, and explain any applicable limitation. We cannot remotely delete files on your device or in a storage service you chose.

5. Advertising and Analytics data

Local reset, clearing storage and uninstalling do not automatically erase data previously received by Google AdMob or, once enabled, Firebase Analytics. Withdraw consent using working privacy controls where available to stop consent-dependent future collection; a privacy request is separate from local deletion. Contact haztartastervezo@gmail.com for applicable access/deletion requests. We assess what can be identified and deleted through the relevant provider tools. An email address alone may not identify a pseudonymous app installation, and aggregated reports may not be attributable to you. We do not promise to delete every Google-held security or advertising record or to erase them immediately. We will explain any lawful retention or identification limit. The Analytics retention period and controls must be published and implemented before that planned integration starts.
