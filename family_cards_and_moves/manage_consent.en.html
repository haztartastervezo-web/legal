This page explains privacy choices for Routine Cards & Moves. Reading it does not change SDK settings or record consent.

Advertising and analytics are separate

Google AdMob is integrated. Non-personalised ads and child age treatment reduce some advertising uses, but do not mean no technical data is processed. A rewarded-video choice is a choice to watch an ad, not consent to every advertising or analytics purpose. Firebase Analytics is planned for predefined button/feature events and can also process automatic SDK data; it is not active in the reviewed source.

Consent and withdrawal

Consent-dependent advertising storage/processing and optional Analytics requiring consent must remain off until valid consent is obtained. The reviewed test source has not connected an in-app advertising privacy form or Analytics consent switch. We do not claim an existing “Manage consent” button works. Before those consent-dependent features are released, adults must be offered the required choices and a way to change or withdraw them. If a privacy-options entry is available in your version, use it; if it is missing, contact support. A support email cannot technically stop an SDK on your device and is not a substitute for an in-app withdrawal control.

The Analytics integration must respect its own collection and consent settings; an advertising choice must not be assumed to configure Analytics automatically. Rejecting personalised advertising also does not by itself mean Analytics is disabled. See the Privacy Policy.

Device controls

Android advertising-ID controls and, where applicable, Apple's App Tracking Transparency controls can restrict particular identifiers or tracking uses. They do not guarantee that all ad requests, technical data or Analytics stop. The reviewed app does not provide a basis to promise an ATT prompt. Adults should manage device permissions and external ad destinations.

Notifications and selected files

Decline or revoke notification permission through device settings, and disable routine reminders in the app. A reminder may show a routine name on the lock screen. You can cancel the system picker without selecting an image, audio file or backup. An imported image or song is a separate app copy; deleting its source does not remove it.

Sharing, local controls and removal

“Save my setup” opens the share sheet with an unencrypted JSON file. Check the destination and manage exported files and device backups separately. The optional PIN limits some parent-area access; it is not verified parental consent. “Start over” does not erase every preference, movement sequence, temporary file or record held by Google. See Data Deletion.

Contact

For privacy requests or unavailable controls, email haztartastervezo@gmail.com. Do not send the PIN or unnecessary child information. Merely installing the app or accepting its terms does not give blanket consent.
