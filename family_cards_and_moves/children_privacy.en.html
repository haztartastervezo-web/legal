Family Cards & Moves is designed for families, including children who use visual routines and movement breaks with an adult's involvement. This notice supplements the Privacy Policy.

What children can use

Routine cards, everyday support KITs, movement activities and progress features work without an online account. There is no public child profile, chat, social feed or developer-operated cloud sync. This version has no ad network, remote analytics or advertising identifier use.

What stays on the device

Custom cards, notes, timetable, goals, selected goal images, imported music and progress can describe a child's life. They are saved locally by the app. Adults should enter only what is necessary and protect the device. Device backup and a setup export you choose to share can take information off the device, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Adult controls and external services

Use the optional PIN to limit access to parent areas and editing. It does not verify adulthood or parental authority and is not a guarantee against every external link or action. Adults should handle file sharing, support messages, store links and device permissions, and supervise review prompts or other links that may appear. External providers may process technical data under their own policies.

No blanket parental consent

Installing the app, setting a PIN, selecting a profile or reading this page is not verifiable parental consent to online collection. The current family features do not ask children to transmit personal information to us. Any future online collection that requires parental consent must be assessed and implemented separately before it begins.

If a child contacts support

Adults should send support requests. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact haztartastervezo@gmail.com. We will remove unnecessary child information and address the request, subject to any legal obligation. We do not use child support messages for marketing or build a child contact list.

Deleting information and safe activities

Follow Data Deletion to reset or remove local information. Choose activities suited to the child's abilities, respect refusal and stop if there is distress. See the Safety Notice. The app does not replace professional care or adult supervision.
