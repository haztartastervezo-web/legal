Routine Cards & Moves is used by adults with children for visual routines and movement breaks. This notice supplements the Privacy Policy.

Family content

Routines, optional child labels, notes, timetable, goals, selected images, imported music, movement preferences and feedback are stored on the device. There is no online family account, public child profile or chat. Exports and device backups can create additional copies; adults should use nicknames and avoid unnecessary health or identifying details.

Advertising and analytics

AdMob is integrated, with native placements in parent areas and adult-initiated rewarded/support videos. Ad requests use non-personalised advertising and child age treatment; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message is shown before ads are requested. Google still processes technical advertising information; this is not an assertion of no online collection. Firebase Analytics usage statistics are off until a parent says yes in the parent area (Settings → Data → Usage statistics). They contain counts, built-in content identifiers and fixed codes, never routine or card names, child labels, notes, mood answers, pictures or typed text; the SDK also processes a pseudonymous app-instance identifier and automatic app/device events, with advertising identifiers switched off. See the Privacy Policy.

Adult controls

Adults should handle adverts, file sharing, permissions, support and external links. Parent areas and an optional PIN do not verify adulthood or parental authority and do not guarantee every external action is gated. A store rating does not replace assessment of the app's actual child users.

Parental consent is separate

Installation, choosing a profile, setting a PIN, watching an ad or reading this notice is not verifiable parental consent to online collection. Child-related advertising flags are not Analytics consent. Any collection requiring parental consent must have an appropriate mechanism before it begins; a written notice alone does not supply one.

Support and deletion

Adults should send support messages. If a child sends personal information, contact haztartastervezo@gmail.com; we remove unnecessary child information subject to legal obligations. We do not use such messages to build child marketing lists. Data Deletion distinguishes local removal, exported copies and Google-held SDK data.

Safe activities

Choose movements suited to the child, respect refusal and stop for discomfort or distress. Read the Safety Notice. The app does not replace professional care or adult supervision.
