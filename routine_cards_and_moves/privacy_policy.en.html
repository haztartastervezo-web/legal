This notice explains information handling in Routine Cards & Moves, a family visual-routine and movement app, and on these legal pages. Adults should read it before setting up the app for a child.

Family content and online services are different. Routines, cards, selected pictures, imported music and family progress are stored locally, without an online family account or developer-operated content-sync server. Google AdMob is integrated for advertising and processes technical data. Google Analytics for Firebase is integrated for usage statistics, but it sends data only after a parent allows it in the app (opt-in). The sections below explain both integrations. This is not a “no data collection” app notice.

The provider and controller for processing under our control is Fütyü-Gábor Enikő (Enila Studio), private individual, 4032 Debrecen, Cívis utca 3., Hungary; haztartastervezo@gmail.com. Enila Studio is a publishing name. Contact us for privacy questions and rights requests.

2. Family information stored on the device

  • Routine names, steps, day-specific schedules, optional child labels, custom card labels, emojis, colours, descriptions and movement selections; best completion times and partially completed routines.
  • Daily completion history and points, weekly goals and selected goal images, school timetable, practice items and notes, favourites and feedback.
  • End-of-routine feedback with date, routine reference and selected response; the app keeps the latest 200 such entries. Saved daily completion history is limited to approximately 60 days.
  • Movement favourites, movement feedback and custom movement sequences, including their names, exercise selections and durations.
  • Music preferences and imported audio copies, reminder settings, language, theme, colour palette, optional parent PIN, dismissed tips, review-prompt counters and the day on which parent extras were unlocked.

Use nicknames for optional child labels and avoid unnecessary diagnoses, health details, addresses or school identifiers. Free text, feedback and pictures may reveal a child's circumstances or health. These family-content fields are not intended as advertising or analytics event parameters. The app has no public child profile, chat or public sharing feed.

3. Selected files, reminders and legal pages

The system picker supplies an image, audio file or backup you choose. Selected images and audio are copied into app storage; deleting the original does not delete the copy. A picker may offer files held by a cloud provider connected to your device. The import function does not upload those files to us. The reviewed app has no camera recording, microphone recording, location-permission or contacts feature.

Routine reminders are scheduled locally, with notification permission requested when you turn them on. They are not server-sent push messages. A routine name may appear on the lock screen. Notification refusal does not stop routine use.

The app downloads legal documents from GitHub Pages and displays their text inside the app. GitHub receives the network request and logs IP addresses for security. These legal pages have no developer-added tracking scripts, advertising, analytics, forms or cookies.

4. Manual exports and device backups

“Save my setup” creates an unencrypted, readable JSON file and opens the system share sheet. It includes routine definitions, optional child labels, custom cards, favourites, ratings, practice items, timetable, weekly goal definitions, routine feedback, profile/music preferences and reminder settings. It omits daily completion history, paused-routine state, imported image/audio files, the parent PIN and the daily parent-unlock entitlement. The separately stored movement favourites, movement feedback and custom movement sequences, language, theme, palette, dismissed-tip and review-prompt preferences are not included.

You choose the destination. Anyone with access to the exported file may read it. The receiving storage, email or messaging service applies its own terms. Temporary export files can remain in app cache. Operating-system backups or device transfers are separate and depend on platform and release settings; they may have different contents. We do not administer them. Delete unwanted copies separately, and remember that restoration can bring information back.

5. Google AdMob advertising

The app integrates Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. The reviewed interface places native advertising in the parent planning and settings areas while the daily parent key is closed. Adults can choose a rewarded video for daily access to parent extras or a separate support video. A completed daily-key video unlocks the extras until the end of that local day; if no video is available, the reviewed implementation also grants access. The support video does not unlock the daily key. The home screen, routine player and movement player do not contain embedded banner or native placements in the reviewed interface. A video chosen by an adult is still advertising.

Ad requests use non-personalised advertising, child age treatment and a general-audience (G) content filter, and the app removes the Android advertising-ID permission from its manifest; on iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission (ATT). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads are requested, and ads are loaded only when Google’s consent status allows it; the choice can be changed later under Parent area → Settings → Privacy and legal → Manage consent, where this option is shown. Depending on the choices and region, Google may serve non-personalised or limited ads, or none. These settings restrict ad serving; they do not mean the SDK sends no data. Google may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, ad requests, impressions, taps and video interactions, technical device/app information and diagnostics, and identifiers available under the SDK configuration, age treatment, platform permissions and privacy choices. We do not promise that every technical identifier is absent.

Purposes include serving and measuring advertising, limiting repeated ads, security and fraud prevention. Do not enter a child's personal information into an advertisement or its destination. Advertisers' destination sites have their own policies. See Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

6. Firebase Analytics: usage statistics, only with your permission

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (Google) to understand how the app’s features are used and to improve them: for example how many routines are set up, which built-in routines, KITs and movement programmes are started and finished, how many steps were done or skipped and how long a routine took, and which parent tools are opened. Collection is switched off by default. The first time the parent area is opened, the app asks “Help us improve the app?”; “Not now” means no. The answer can be changed at any time under Parent area → Settings → Data → Usage statistics. Without a yes, the app sends no Analytics events.

What the app sends after a yes: predefined event names with numbers (counts, step numbers, durations in seconds), yes/no flags and fixed codes. These are identifiers of built-in routines, KITs and movement programmes (own routines and programmes appear only as “custom”), the KIT rating as a fixed code (helped / partly / not now), that the end-of-routine question was answered (not the answer), the outcome of rewarded videos, backup / restore / start-over actions, rating-prompt and other-app card interactions, a summary of the setup as counts (for example the number of routines, own pictures, reminders, weekly goals and of different child labels) and three user properties (routine-count range, whether an own routine exists, whether reminders are on). We never send routine or card names, child labels, notes, school entries, mood answers, pictures, music files, the parent PIN, typed text or dates, and we set no user ID.

The Analytics SDK also processes, automatically, a pseudonymous app-instance identifier (it is not anonymous), app-lifecycle and session events (such as first open, session start and engagement time), app version, operating system, device model, language and country, and approximate location derived from the IP address; Google does not show us the IP address. Collection of the advertising ID, the Android ID (SSAID) and the iOS IDFV and advertising-personalisation signals are switched off in the app configuration, and on iOS the Analytics library without advertising-identifier support is used. Consent Mode: analytics storage follows your answer; ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are always denied. We do not use Google Signals or links to Google Ads. See Firebase privacy information and Analytics data collection.

If you switch usage statistics off later, collection stops at once and the app resets the app-instance identifier and the Analytics data stored on the device; events already sent stay with Google until the retention period in section 8 ends. Development (debug) builds send nothing.

9. Choices and deletion

“Start over” resets the main family setup and attempts to remove imported audio and goal images, but it is not a complete wipe. The parent PIN, appearance/language settings, tips and review-prompt preferences, separately stored movement preferences/custom sequences the daily parent-key entitlement and the usage-statistics answer can remain. A recovery copy of unreadable family data and temporary exports may remain. Clear app storage, or delete the app with its data, for a complete local reset. External copies and data already sent to advertising or analytics services require separate handling. See Privacy Choices and Permissions and Data Deletion.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message is shown before ads are requested, and “Manage consent” in Settings reopens it. Usage statistics are sent only after the parent’s yes and can be switched off under Settings → Data. Installing the app, opening a parent area, setting a PIN or choosing a video is not consent. Contact us if a privacy control does not work.

10. Children and security

Adults set up and supervise family use, including children using routine cards and movements. A parent-only screen or a store age rating does not by itself prove that children never use the app. The optional PIN is a convenience barrier, not verified parental consent or encrypted storage. Child-related advertising settings do not establish consent for separate Analytics. See Children's Privacy and Parent Information.

Local preferences and files rely on operating-system protections; the app adds no database encryption and its setup export is not encrypted. Use a device passcode and current software. No system guarantees complete security.
