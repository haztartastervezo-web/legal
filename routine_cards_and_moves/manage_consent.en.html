This page explains privacy choices for Routine Cards & Moves. Reading it does not change SDK settings or record consent.

Advertising and analytics are separate

Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase are two separate integrations with two separate choices. Non-personalised ads and child age treatment reduce some advertising uses, but do not mean no technical data is processed. A rewarded-video choice is a choice to watch an ad, not consent to every advertising or analytics purpose. An advertising choice does not switch usage statistics on, and switching usage statistics off does not change advertising choices.

Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

Usage statistics are off by default. The first time the parent area is opened, the app asks “Help us improve the app?”: “Yes, I’ll help” switches them on, “Not now” leaves them off. You can change the answer at any time under Parent area → Settings → Data → Usage statistics. When it is off, the app sends no Analytics events; switching it off later also resets the app-instance identifier and the Analytics data stored on the device. Events sent before that stay with Google for the retention period stated in the Privacy Policy (2 months for event-level data). The answer is stored only on this device; it is not part of “Save my setup” and “Start over” keeps it.

Advertising consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown at start-up before ads are requested, and ads load only when Google’s consent status allows it. To change or withdraw your choice later, open Parent area → Settings → Privacy and legal → Manage consent; this item appears where Google requires it. Depending on your choices and region, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads, or none; the app’s parent extras do not depend on accepting personalised advertising. Outside these regions the message is not shown, and ads are always requested as non-personalised with child age treatment.

Device controls

The app removes the Android advertising-ID permission and does not ask for Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission, so it does not request access to the advertising identifier for tracking. Android and iOS privacy settings can restrict further identifiers. They do not stop every technical ad request. Adults should manage device permissions and external ad destinations.

Notifications and selected files

Decline or revoke notification permission through device settings, and disable routine reminders in the app. A reminder may show a routine name on the lock screen. You can cancel the system picker without selecting an image, audio file or backup. An imported image or song is a separate app copy; deleting its source does not remove it.

Sharing, local controls and removal

“Save my setup” opens the share sheet with an unencrypted JSON file. Check the destination and manage exported files and device backups separately. The optional PIN limits some parent-area access; it is not verified parental consent. “Start over” does not erase every preference, movement sequence, temporary file or record held by Google. See Data Deletion.

Contact

For privacy requests or unavailable controls, email haztartastervezo@gmail.com. Do not send the PIN or unnecessary child information. Merely installing the app or accepting its terms does not give blanket consent.
