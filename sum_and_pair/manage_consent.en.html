This page supplements the Privacy Policy and summarises the choices the App offers and how you can change them at any time.

1. Which choices the App asks for

  • Ads (EEA, UK, Switzerland): at start-up, if your region requires it, the App shows Google's standard consent form through the User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management platform certified by Google. It covers storing/accessing information on your device and ad personalisation by Google and its ad partners.
  • Tracking (iOS only): Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt asks whether the App and its ad partners may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Notifications: the operating system asks whether the App may send you reminders.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase runs on the basis of our legitimate interest and is not controlled by the ad consent form. You can object to it at any time by writing to haztartastervezo@gmail.com.

2. How to change your choices

  • Ad consent: open the App's settings and tap “Manage consent” (privacy settings) to reopen Google's form and change your choice. The option appears where Google requires it.
  • Tracking (iOS): iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking — you can allow or deny tracking for the App at any time without reinstalling it.
  • Notifications: in the App's settings or in your device's notification settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. What happens if you decline

The App keeps working fully, including rewards for rewarded ads, as long as ads can be shown. Without consent, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads, or in some cases no ad at all. Declining ATT on iOS means the IDFA is not available; other choices made in Google's form still apply.

4. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time as described above. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
