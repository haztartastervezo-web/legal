Manage Consent

App: Quick Daily Fitness (com.enila.quickdailyfitness) · Developer: Enila Studio · Last updated: October 11, 2026

This page describes the consent and permission choices that actually exist in the Quick Daily Fitness app (the "App"), what each one controls, and how you can change or withdraw it.

1. Google ad-consent dialog (UMP) — EEA, UK, Switzerland

What it controls: whether Google AdMob and the ad partners listed in the dialog may store or access information on your device and use your data for personalized ads and ad measurement — and, through Google Consent Mode, whether Google Analytics for Firebase may store an analytics identifier on your device and use ad-related signals.

When it appears: at startup, if Google determines that consent is required in your region. The Mobile Ads SDK is only started after this step.

If you decline or make limited choices: depending on your choices, Google may show non-personalized ads, limited ads, or no ads at all. Declining does not block any workout feature of the App.

How to change it: More → "About us" → "Manage consent". This item is shown where Google requires a way to revisit your choice; if you do not see it, consent is not required for you in your region.

2. App Tracking Transparency (ATT) — iOS only

The current version does not show Apple's tracking prompt and does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If a future version uses ads that may track you across other companies' apps and websites, iOS will ask first — separately from the Google dialog — and you will be able to change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. This page will be updated then.

3. Advertising ID — Android

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings (e.g. Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device). After deletion, apps no longer receive it for personalized advertising.

4. Notifications

What it controls: whether the App may show the optional daily workout reminder. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device; no data is sent to a server.

How to change it: turn the reminder off in the App's settings, or withdraw notification permission in your device settings.

5. Analytics and crash reports (Android version)

Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase starts with identifier storage denied (Google Consent Mode). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it stores an analytics identifier only if you consent in the Google dialog (section 1) to storing and/or accessing information on your device; withdrawing that choice via "Manage consent" takes effect immediately. Elsewhere it is enabled on the basis of our legitimate interest — you can object by email.

Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics runs on the basis of our legitimate interest and is not controlled by the dialog. You can object at any time by emailing haztartastervezo@gmail.com.

In the current iOS version these Firebase services are not activated.

6. Effect of withdrawing consent

Withdrawing consent applies from that moment on; it does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before. Withdrawing or declining never blocks the App's workout features.
