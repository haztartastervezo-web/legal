Privacy Policy

App: Quick Daily Fitness (Android package / iOS bundle ID: com.enila.quickdailyfitness) · Developer: Enila Studio · Last updated: October 11, 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the Quick Daily Fitness mobile app (the "App"), a short daily home-workout app, processes, why, with whom it is shared, and what rights you have. This policy is for information only; where your consent is required, the App asks for it separately.

In short: Your workouts, fitness-test results, journal notes, mood entries and settings are stored only on your device — we do not receive them. The App has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or password. The App contains Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) with Google's consent dialog, and in the Android version it uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These Google services process pseudonymous technical identifiers (for example an app-instance ID or advertising ID) and device data — this is not anonymous data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Analytics follows your choices in Google's consent dialog. The current version does not ask for Apple's tracking permission (ATT) and does not access the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA).

1. Controller and contact

The controller is Enila Studio, an independent app developer based in the European Union. For any privacy question or request, write to: haztartastervezo@gmail.com.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Workout history: completed sessions, dates, durations, progress in programs and weekly themes, milestones reached
  • Fitness test results: number of jumping jacks in the test, the calculated score and level, and the test dates
  • Workout adaptation: exercises you excluded, your difficulty setting and your per-exercise feedback (e.g. "too hard"), saved exercises
  • Daily journal: the free-text notes and mood emoji you enter
  • Settings: session length, reminder time, theme and appearance of the on-screen trainer, sound, re-test frequency
  • Temporary unlocks earned by watching a rewarded ad (e.g. a period without banner ads or extra content) and when they expire, and the state of the "rate the app" prompt (e.g. when it was last shown)

All of this information — including fitness-test results, workout feedback, mood and journal notes — is stored only in the App's private storage on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party; we do not process it.

The App currently has no "delete all data" button. You can delete individual journal notes in the App; to delete all local data, use your device's settings (Android: Settings → Apps → Quick Daily Fitness → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the App. Note: if you use your device's own backup (e.g. Android or iCloud device backup), the operating system may include app data in that backup under your device settings — this is outside our control.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is initialized at startup after the consent step (section 5). The App may show banner ads and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to temporarily unlock extra content or a period without banner ads. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To deliver, limit, measure and — if permitted — personalize ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process: the advertising ID (Android; the current iOS version does not request ATT, so the IDFA is not available); IP address and approximate location derived from it; device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version); and ad interactions (impressions, clicks). Google acts under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Usage analytics and crash reports (Firebase, Android version)

In the Android version, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. In the current iOS version these Firebase services are not activated.

  • Analytics: the App records whether the "rate the app" prompt was shown and which answer was chosen (rate / I have an idea / not now). Firebase also collects automatically recorded data such as a randomly generated app-instance ID, first open, sessions and screens used, device model, OS version, app version, language/region, approximate location (derived from the IP address); it may also receive the advertising ID. Your workouts, test results, journal and mood are not sent to Analytics.
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): if the App crashes or hits an error, a report is sent with the error details and stack trace, device model, OS version, app version, the time of the crash and a Crashlytics installation identifier.

These identifiers are pseudonymous: we do not link them to your name or contact details, but they are personal data under the GDPR.

Google Consent Mode: Analytics starts with the storage of analytics and advertising identifiers denied. Where consent is not required, it is then enabled. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it follows your choices in the consent dialog (section 5): analytics storage only if you consent to storing and/or accessing information on your device, and ad-related signals only with the corresponding ad consents. Without that consent, Analytics receives only limited signals without an analytics identifier stored on your device. Crash reports are sent based on our legitimate interest, independently of the dialog; you may object to them (section 1).

5. Consent and tracking choices

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: on startup the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform, to ask for your choices about storing/accessing information on your device and ad personalization. The Mobile Ads SDK is only started after this step. Depending on your choices, Google may show personalized ads, non-personalized ads, limited ads, or no ads at all. You can change your choice at any time in More → "About us" → "Manage consent" (shown where Google requires it).
  • iOS – App Tracking Transparency (ATT): the current version does not show Apple's tracking prompt and does not access the IDFA. If a future version uses ads that may track you across other companies' apps and websites, iOS will ask for your permission first; ATT is separate from the Google consent dialog, and this policy will be updated.
  • Notifications: the App asks for permission to show notifications for the optional daily workout reminder.

Details: Manage Consent.

6. Other functions

  • Daily reminder: if you turn it on, the reminder is scheduled locally on your device by the operating system; no data is sent to a server for this.
  • Contact and feedback by email: if you write to us via "Help and feedback", "Contact / support" or the "I have an idea" answer of the rate prompt, your email app opens; we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you.
  • Share and store links: "Share" opens your device's system share sheet with a store link; "Rate us" and "Our other apps" open the app store. We do not receive data about these actions.
  • In-app update (Android): the App asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. This is handled by Google Play, not our servers.

The App does not access your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, does not count your steps or read motion/activity sensor data, does not connect to Health Connect or Apple Health, and sends no push notifications from a server.

8. Recipients (processors and independent providers)

  • Google (Firebase: Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics) — analytics and crash reports, as our processor. Firebase privacy information
  • Google AdMob and, where applicable, ad partners displayed in the consent dialog — ad delivery; Google acts partly as an independent controller under its own terms.
  • Google Play (Android) — in-app update check, under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal data for money. We do not share data with any other third parties except where required by law.

9. Retention

  • Local data: until you delete it, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics data: retained by Google according to the data-retention setting of our Google Analytics property and Google's own retention policies; aggregated reports may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports: retained by Google according to Firebase Crashlytics' retention rules (Firebase privacy information).
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under Google's retention policies (policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted unless we must keep them by law.

Uninstalling the App deletes local data but does not by itself delete data already received by Google; see section 10.

10. Your rights and deletion

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interest (including analytics and crash reports), and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Hungary: NAIH, naih.hu).

Because the App has no accounts, we usually cannot identify you from your email request alone. To help with analytics or crash data, we may ask for the app-instance ID or other details. You can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings, and withdraw ad and analytics consent via "Manage consent".

US residents: depending on your state (e.g. California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to know, access, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of "sale"/"sharing" or targeted advertising. Use of the advertising ID for personalized ads may count as "sharing" under some state laws; you can opt out by limiting ad tracking / deleting your advertising ID in device settings, declining personalized ads in the consent dialog, or contacting us. We do not sell personal information for money.

11. International transfers

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (where Google is certified) and/or Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

12. Children

The App is a general-audience fitness app; it is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families program. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us personal data (e.g. by email), contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Local data is stored in the App's private storage on your device; protect your device with a screen lock to keep your journal and workout data private.

14. Automated decision-making

The App adapts your daily workout (pace, difficulty, exercise selection) on your device based on your fitness-test result and feedback. This has no legal or similarly significant effect, and you can change it at any time. Ad selection by Google may be automated.

15. Changes

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. The current version is always published at this address, with the date at the top.
